Figma Website Prototype

Streamline Studios — Isaiah Evans

A styled, clickable Figma prototype for Streamline Studios, a Charlotte-based automotive photography and videography business. The prototype has three pages (Home, Packages and Contact) and starts on the Home page.

View the clickable prototype View the Figma design file

What's in the prototype

  • Visual styling: dark color palette with a gold accent, Bebas Neue headings and Inter body text, and car photography.
  • Reusable components: Nav Bar, Button (Primary, Outline and Dark styles), Package Card and Footer.
  • Navigation: Home, Packages and Contact links in the nav bar on every page.
  • Extra interactions: the "View Packages" button opens Packages, and the "Book a Shoot" and "Book This Package" buttons open Contact.
  • Wireframes: the low-fidelity versions are on the "Wireframes (Low-Fi)" page of the Figma file.
